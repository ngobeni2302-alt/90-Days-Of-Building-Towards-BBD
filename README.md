# 90 Days of Java — Building Towards BBD

> A Software Engineering student at WeThinkCode_ chasing his dream.

### The Story

I got rejected from BBD.

It hurt, because I'm a huge fan of what BBD does — the culture, the tech, the haircuts story, hiring problem solvers not just coders.

After the email, I sat down and researched what BBD actually looks for. I even met Lucky who works at BBD, and he told me:

**"If you want to work at BBD, you need to know how to cook. You need to bring something to the table."**

That stuck.

BBD chases excellence and greatness, so that's what I'm going to offer them.

Before you can be a chef, you need to understand the basics: chopping, switching on the stove, multitasking, and working under pressure to make sure the food is not only cooked, but served well.

This 90-day project is me learning how to cook.

### The Challenge

**90 Days of Java, starting [05 October 2025].**
Coding every single day for 90 days, documenting struggles, wins, and lessons.

- Phase 1 (Days 1-30): Java Core — variables, loops, strings, arrays, methods
- Phase 2 (Days 31-60): OOP + Collections — the BBD must-knows
- Phase 3 (Days 61-90): Data Structures, Algorithms & Interview Prep

Not for pity. For growth. And to prove to myself and to BBD that I belong there.
